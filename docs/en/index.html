Introduction

Grion is an online RPG set in a world of swords and magic, where you hunt monsters with friends, complete quests, and grow stronger. It's free to play during Early Access (Windows 11/10). If the full release becomes paid, everyone who played before then keeps playing for free. Defeating monsters outside town earns you EXP and Grim (money), and you can reach up to level 60.

Early Access: For now, you can play up to Lv60 and up to the summit of the Sky Corridor. Lv61–70 and the "Sky Capital Arc" will become playable in the full release.
The Grion StoryThis world is written in a single Grimoire floating in the sky. A tale of the Seven Crowns and a city that vanished into the sky, with plans for sequels beyond Lv60. Read it before you play, and towns and monsters will look different to you.

Before You Start

  1. Download the installer from itch.io, then open GrimoireOnline_Setup.exe and click "Next". If you see "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" → "Run anyway" (this appears because the app was made by an individual developer).
  2. Open "Grimoire Online" on your desktop, choose a login name and password, and press "Create Account".
  3. Create your character. Choose from 5 races, each in either gender, and 6 jobs (you can change jobs in town as often as you like later).

Race

You can enjoy the game with any race you choose. Race differences aren't large enough to determine how strong a job is. Feel free to choose by looks or preference.

The + and - stat values are racial modifiers added to a job's base values. HP and MP use percentage modifiers on the job's values. A job with 0 MP stays at 0 even with an MP modifier.

From the second time on, just click the desktop icon. The game checks for a new version at launch and updates itself.

Controls at a Glance (All You Need to Get Moving)

The most important flow: Get close → select the target with Tab → press Enter to talk or examine (for monsters, choose "Attack" in the Command Menu). When nothing is open to close, Esc opens the Main Menu, and , (comma) opens the Controls List. There is no mouse cursor.
Beginner Guidance: Guidance suited to the situation appears at the top of the screen when you first use a control or reach a milestone in your adventure. There are 31 scenes in all, including the Main Menu, Controls List, map, Quest List, chat recipient, auto-gathering, Grica, unlocking Auto-Hunt Mode, receiving the Kururu Whistle, and entering a dungeon for the first time. You can reread guidance you've already seen from "Beginner Guidance" in the Main Menu.

Talking to NPCs & Shopping

Close windows and prompts with Esc.

Walk up to an NPC, select them with Tab, and press Enter. In shops, choose an item and press Enter to buy; to sell, move to your own bag column with ←→. An NPC with a "!" over their head has a quest ("!!" means a Proof of Bond quest, and "?" means you can report back or the quest is in progress).

First Steps (Lv1–10)

  1. Start with your first quest. "Slime Extermination" (5 Slimes in North Rockhill) is already accepted when you begin (check it in the Quest Log). It is given by the Townsperson in the residential quarter on the west side of town. The Guard, Miner, and Traveling Merchant in the same square, and the Shady Man lurking behind a house in the castle quarter to the west, also offer low-level quests.
  2. Buy 2–3 Potions at the item shop. Potions (restore 60 HP) are your lifeline early on. The Novice's Blessing reduces the damage you take at Lv1–3, but getting jumped by two monsters at once is still dangerous.
  3. Head out the east gate and hunt Slimes (Lv1) and Chickens (Lv1) in North Rockhill. Select with Tab, then Enter → "Attack". Fight one at a time, and when your HP drops below half, sit and rest with H or go back to town. Stay here until Lv4–5.
  4. At Lv3–5, move on to Bats, Goblins, and Scorpions. Bats and Scorpions "react to footsteps", so approach at a walk instead of running and they're less likely to ambush you. Goblins "attack on sight", so circle around behind them.
  5. Report your quest to earn Grim, gear, and your first companion. Equip the "Slime Extermination" reward, the Adventurer's Ring (HP/MP+10), right away (P). When you report it, a Bond joins you: Warriors, Monks, and Rogues get the healer "Cleric Sera"; fragile Sorcerers, Clerics, and Spellswords get the tank "Warrior Rio". Rio uses Taunt to draw enemies away from you. Select yourself with Z, then Enter → "Call Bond" to call them. Things get much easier from here.
  6. At Lv6–9, take on Goblin Sentries and Cactuses, then head to the entrance of South Rockhill. Finishing "Taking Down the Goblin Leaders" (Guild Receptionist) starts the Adventurers' Guild quest series.
  7. From Lv10, gain more companions with Proofs of Bond. Mentor Elna's "Proof of Bond: Healing" (Lv10, 8 Armabees in South Rockhill) lets you call "Cleric Sera", and Drillmaster Dorg's "Proof of Bond: Blade" (Lv15) lets you call "Warrior Rio". The Proof for the Bond you got from your first quest counts as already done. Until you reach that Proof's level (Lv10 for Sera, Lv15 for Rio), you can't call your first Bond together with other Bonds.
If you fall: From Lv9, you lose part of the EXP you've earned toward your current level (your level never goes down. Nothing is lost at Lv8 or below, or in the Full Moon Arena). Choose "Revive at Return Sigil" to return to the last Return Sigil you touched (the town at first). If a Cleric raises you with Resurrect (Lv25), you get part of the lost EXP back.

What's in Town

  • Return Sigil: Select it with Tab and touch it with Enter to make it your revival point.
  • Inn (Sleeping Cat Inn): Talk to the landlady to fully restore HP and MP.
  • Item Shop, Weapon Shop, Armor Shop: Gear for the early game up to Lv10, Potions, fishing rods, and bait.
  • The Rockhill fountain: At the top of every hour it shoots up for about 30 seconds, and at 6 a.m., noon, and 6 p.m. it becomes a grand fountain. At night it glows (one hour in the game is 144 seconds of real time).
  • Warehouse Keeper: Store items you aren't using. Mailbox: Send items and letters to other players. An envelope symbol appears on the screen when you have uncollected mail. Auction Clerk: Buy and sell items with other players (/ah).
  • Job Instructor: Change your job. Each job levels up separately.
  • Guild Receptionist / Drillmaster Dorg / Mentor Elna / Pomly / Kururu Stable Master / Corridor Guide: Explained in their own sections.
Log in every day to receive a 200 Grim Login Bonus on your first entry of the day (once per day, shared with the Web Portal).

Milestones to Remember

  • Lv1: Report your first quest, "Slime Extermination", and a Bond (Sera or Rio) joins you.
  • Lv10: Rings and earrings. The Proof of Bond for Sera. The fishing quest. You can challenge Great Mine Lord Dorgan (Lv9).
  • Lv12–16: The Kururu Stable Master's 3 quests earn you the Kururu Whistle. Ride with V and travel faster than running.
  • Lv18–20: The Pomly's quests unlock your Sub Job. You can layer half the strength of a second job on top of your Main Job (e.g. Warrior/Cleric can cast Heal).
  • Lv30: Quests to unlock the 5 Expert Jobs. Auto-Hunt Mode becomes available (Ctrl+Shift+Q or /ai).
  • Lv50: The "Hero's Credentials" quest. The way to Shadow Rockhill opens. Shadowbronze through Hero gear.
  • Lv60: The cap (during Early Access; Lv61–70 come in the full release). The title "Veteran Adventurer".

Sub Job (Lv18+)

Complete the town Pomly's quests "Proof of Flame" (Gara-Hei), "Proof of Earth" (Soldier Crawler), and "Proof of Water" (Princess Toad), then "The Pomly's Trial" (Serpent Priest Shezal), and you can set a Sub Job in addition to your Main Job. Your Sub Job works at up to half your Main Job's level, and you can use its spells and abilities within that range.

On servers using Insights, train your Sub Job as your Main Job to levels 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60 to unlock six tiers. One action unlocks at tier two and another at tier four. Active tiers are capped at your Main Job level plus 10 (configurable by the server). Levels 1–9 grant no effects; the display shows the levels remaining until the First Insight. Level matching preserves tiers but scales their effects by the ratio of matched to original Main Job level. Servers using the original rules retain the half-Main-Job-level cap.

Bonds — Companions Who Fight Beside You

Bonds are companions who fight beside you whenever you call them. You can call up to 5, and their strength scales to your level. With a healer, a tank, and damage dealers, you can take on stronger monsters and even Lords. To unlock a Bond, complete the "Proof of Bond" quests from Drillmaster Dorg (front-line Bonds) and Mentor Elna (spellcaster Bonds) in town.

  • Call: Select yourself with Z, then Enter → "Call Bond". Or press Ctrl+Q to call in missing Bonds one by one (you can't call them during combat, while riding a Kururu, in the Arena, or in the Sky Corridor).
  • Dismiss: "Dismiss Bond" in the same Command Menu. Bonds can come with you into dungeons, but inside Shadow Rockhill you can't call replacements.
  • Each Bond thinks for itself. Tanks draw enemies with Taunt, Clerics heal based on how fast HP is dropping, and Sorcerers put one enemy to sleep when there are two.
  • When a Bond falls, the enemies that were targeting it head for you, its nearby master.

Combat Basics

Reading Monster Behavior

  • Attacks on sight: Comes at you once you're in view. Circle around from behind.
  • Reacts to footsteps: Notices you if you run up to it. Walk and you can slip past (riding a Kururu makes you easier to notice).
  • Reacts to spellcasting: Attacks if you cast magic nearby. Move away before healing.
  • Passive: Won't attack unless you strike first. Take them on one at a time without worry.

A monster someone else is fighting has a gray name and can't be stolen ("Another adventurer is fighting it"). Lords stand out with gold-orange names and a ★.

In the field, adventurers from the Adventurers' Guild (NPCs) are also out hunting. Look for <Guild Adventurer> above their nameplate. They won't answer if you talk to them, but you'll see them fighting in the same hunting grounds.

Parties

Parties hold up to 6 players. Select someone with Tab, then Enter → "Invite to Party". EXP is shared based on the highest-level member of the party, so with a big level gap you'll get almost nothing. In that case, the leader starts a Level Match (matches everyone to one member's level; Lv10+). Select party members with F1–F6 to heal or buff them, and use E (Assist) to target the same enemy.

Recovery & Supplies

  • Sit with H to recover HP and MP. Moving stops it.
  • Buy Potions, Mana Potions, Antidotes, and Voice Tonics at the item shop. Use them from "Items" in the Command Menu or with /item item name.
  • Food (Grilled Mutton, Salt-Grilled Fish, and more) grants a buff for a while when eaten. Get it by crafting in Veil Village or at the Fish Wholesaler.

Auto-Hunt Mode

Once any job reaches Lv30, you can use Auto-Hunt Mode with Ctrl+Shift+Q (or /ai). Your character walks to a hunting ground suited to your level, calls Bonds, hunts monsters, and flees when things get dangerous. You can also choose whether it uses potions under "Auto-Hunt Potions" in the Main Menu. It can't be used in the trial (Shadow Rockhill) or the Sky Corridor.

Kururu Derby (Kururu Races)

Kururu are birds you can ride. Kururu now look like emus and wear a riding seat and a harness, but no footrests. In the game, Kururu Derby races are held regularly; buy tickets, pick the winners, and your Grim grows. Payouts use a pari-mutuel system, split among the winners (20% house cut).

In the Game

  1. Talk to the Kururu Ticket Booth in town to see the race card and odds for the next race.
  2. Choose a bet type: Win (pick the 1st-place finisher), Place (wins if your pick finishes in the top 3), or Kururu Quinella (pick 2 gates and call 1st and 2nd in any order). Bets come in units of 100 Grim.
  3. Buy before betting closes. Races are broadcast on the big screen in town, and winnings are paid out automatically when the results are in.

Racing on Your Own Kururu

Get the Kururu Whistle from the Kururu Stable Master's quests (Lv12–16) and you can ride a Kururu with V. Riding speed starts at 1.5× walking speed and rises as Affection grows, reaching 2.0× at Affection Lv6. Talk to the Kururu Race Desk in town while mounted to take on a time trial around North Rockhill (no prizes, just records).

Because Kururu speed was rebalanced, personal bests and standings for the time trial were reset once in the update that made this change.

Raising Your Kururu

A character who doesn't yet have a Kururu Whistle receives one Kururu Trial Ride Ticket through Guild Mail. Use it from your bag to ride a Kururu for a set amount of logged-in time. The remaining time appears on screen and doesn't decrease while you're logged out. You can earn the whistle through the Kururu Stable Master's quests.

  • Affection: Rises when you give your Kururu its favorite herbs. Select a favorite herb in your Bag window and choose "Use"; a confirmation window shows the item name, the Affection EXP it will actually add, and today's remaining allowance after feeding. Choose "Yes" to feed it to your Kururu. When it is full, "Yes" cannot be selected. Its favorites are Healing Herb, Fragrant Herb, Moonglow Herb, and Stardust Herb; the rarer the herb, the more it warms to you. It dislikes Mystic Herb and won't eat it. There is a daily limit on how much you can feed it.
  • Kururu level: Rises with the distance you ride. Running around in circles in the same spot barely counts. There is also a daily limit on how much distance counts.
  • Status window: In the Status window (C), press Tab to switch in order: Stats → Kururu → Titles. On the "Kururu" tab you can check Affection, Kururu level, current speed, feed given today, and distance run today (only if you have the Kururu Whistle).

Hover Run and Gliding (Wind Harness)

If you have the "Wind Harness", press Space once while riding your Kururu to float a little above the ground as you run (Hover Run); press it again to land. While hovering you can cross low ledges, and your speed rises by about 10%.

Hold Space while falling to glide; let go to fall normally. On a controller, the jump button (LT) does the same.

Hover Run and gliding use "wind". You have up to about 30 seconds of wind; it refills while you run on the ground, and when it runs out you land. They can't be used in town, Veil Village, dungeons, or the Sky Corridor. To find out where to get the Wind Harness, see "New Adventures with Your Kururu" below.

Win 3, 5, or 10 Kururu Derby bets in a row to earn the titles "Bold Bettor", "Chosen of the Kururu", and "Legendary Eye".

Fishing, Gathering, Crafting & Grica

Fishing

Buy a Wooden Fishing Rod (main) and bait (such as Earthworms; sub) at the Item Shop. Press N (or /fish) by the water to automatically equip a rod and bait from your bag and ready your line. After each attempt, you stay ready with that gear equipped; while the result card is shown, press Enter to close it and cast again. The same bait is replenished automatically if more remains in your bag; if only other bait remains, a selection window opens. While ready, press , (the Controls List key) to change bait. Press N again or Esc, walk, enter combat, fall, change zones, or log out to stop and restore your previous gear. Different bait catches different fish (Earthworm = small fish, River Shrimp = medium fish, Glowfly = big or rare fish, Phantom Fish Paste = legendary fish), and rods grow stronger from Wooden to Artisan to the legendary Dragonwhisker. Angler Ved's quest in town (Lv10, 10 fish) rewards the Artisan Fishing Rod. The Fish Wholesaler buys catches in lots of 10; the daily special pays more, and the buying limit resets in about a week.

The Web "Waterside Fishing Grounds"

The Web Portal's "Waterside Fishing Grounds" plays differently from fishing in the game. Use 1 Fishing Ticket to cast (N or "Cast"), then go through these steps.

  1. Read the float: Don't press while the float only twitches; wait (hook too early and it gets away). When it sinks hard, hook it with Space or Enter. Hook too late and it also gets away.
  2. The fight: While the fish rests, hold Space/Enter to reel. When it runs, tilt the rod opposite to its run (←→); when it dives, raise the rod (↑); when it jumps, lower it (↓). A small sign appears before the fish moves.
  3. Watch the Tension meter (Slack · Good · About to snap) and the Distance left (in m) on screen, and land the fish by pulling it all the way in while the tension is "Good". The line snaps if you pull too hard, and also if you do nothing.
  4. Result: The size (cm) of your catch is shown, with a notice when you beat your personal best. The fish goes into your in-game bag.
  • Small fish can be caught just by reeling and tilting the rod left and right. Diving and jumping start with medium-sized fish.
  • You use the best rod in your bag (or a Loaner Rod if you have none). Choose one bait from your bag to use; better bait hooks bigger fish. Big fish beyond your Rod Power snap the line almost every time, however skilled you are. Even without bait, you can fish with Loaner Bait (like an Earthworm), which never runs out.
  • Fishing Skill is one and the same in the game and on the Web. Raising it in either place increases the same number.
  • On a phone, use the on-screen buttons (Hook/Reel and the arrows) for the same controls.

"Now" and "To do" at the Top of the Screen

While you fish, two lines of guidance appear at the top of the screen.

  • Now: What is happening (watching the float, the float twitched, the float sank hard, the fish ran right, and so on). During the fight, the tension and the distance to go are added at the end.
  • To do: The one thing to press right now, with the name of the key (on a phone, the name of the on-screen button). If you're unsure, just press what this line says.

The color of "To do" shows how urgent it is.

  • Teal: Wait. Don't press yet.
  • Gold: Press (cast, reel, and so on).
  • Red: Press right away (the float sank, the fish is about to move, the rod is tilted the wrong way, or the line is about to snap).

While your skill is low (up to 30), a big arrow also appears in the pond, and the edge of the button to press lights up. At skill 31, the arrows stop appearing. Beginner Guidance tells you once shortly before that (at 28–30) and once more when you reach 31.

Expert Mode

Check "Expert mode" at the very bottom of the control panel to play without the "Now" and "To do" guides and the arrows. It's for players who are used to fishing and want to read the float and the fish with their own eyes.

  • The tension, the distance, the fish's movements (such as "Running right!"), and the result still appear as before.
  • The fish you can catch, their size, and the rewards don't change. Only the guides disappear.
  • Your choice is saved to your character and shared between fishing on the Web and in the game. It starts off (guides shown).

Grimoire Cards (Grica)

Grica "Magic Circle" is a game of capturing cards across 7 points: 6 on the outer ring plus the Core in the center. Any cards you've collected can be used as-is. Build a 4-card deck in the B Card Album, select an opponent, and challenge them with X. To play the person across from you, sit at a table and challenge them.

  • What matters on a card is its power (1–10) and element. On the outer ring, your card is compared with the opponent's cards on both neighboring points and at the far end of the star lines; if yours is stronger, you capture them. Equal strength can't capture.
  • The Cycle runs Fire → Wind → Thunder → Water → Ice → Earth → Fire. Attacking the next element in the cycle gives +2. Light and Dark get +2 against each other and +1 against the other 6 elements. Circle Blessing: +1 if the card's element matches the outer point it's placed on. Defending cards get the blessing too.
  • After each side places 3 cards on the outer ring in turn, the side holding fewer outer points (or the second player, if tied) places the last card on the Core. The Core is compared with all 6 outer points. Finally, count 1 point per outer point and 2 for the Core; the higher score wins. Cards left in hand don't count.
  • Circle Inscriptions change the rules of a match. They're set per opponent and table, so read the description on the board before placing. The Preview before placing shows the strengths being compared and which cards you can capture.
  • If you're new, talk to the Grica Teacher in the Grica Parlor and try "Your First Magic Circle". The teacher explains out loud. In the Web version, you can play the same seven lessons from "How to Play".

Board controls: ↑↓ for your hand, A/D for the outer ring, W for the Core, and S to go back to the outer ring. Enter shows the Preview; press it again to place. P toggles the Preview, and pressing Esc during a match asks whether to resign (Enter to resign, Esc to continue). In the Web version, tap a card and a spot, check the Preview, then confirm.

What Opens Up at High Level

Sky Corridor (Anyone)

Talk to the Corridor Guide in town to be sent to a tower you climb by jumping, with no fighting. Falling won't kill you; you just return to the highest Brazier you've passed. A 10-minute time trial starts when you enter. The Braziers along the way only mark your progress; Grim is awarded only when you reach the summit. Your first clear of the day pays 300–600 Grim (250 Grim plus a speed bonus: under 3 min +350, under 5 min +300, under 8 min +175, under 10 min +50), and later clears pay 10%. Rewards are limited to 3 clears a day, up to 1,500 Grim per day (you can keep climbing after that). Reaching the top earns the title "Skyrunner", and doing it in under 3 minutes earns "Skysoarer". Your jumps are higher only inside the Corridor. Bonds and Kururu can't come in.

The course changes daily (switching at 4 a.m. every morning), rotating in this order: Weathervane Spiral (blue sky and stone footholds) → Crimson Cloud Isles (sunset clouds; shuttling platforms, rising-and-falling platforms, and bounce pads) → Starcrossing Skybridge (night sky; star platforms, platforms that appear and vanish, and a single-plank bridge). Crimson Cloud and Starcrossing are harder: every jump differs in distance and height, with round, square, triangular, and hexagonal footholds, spots where you drop down before climbing, and forks that dead-end. Only the bottom two tiers have a route anyone can climb. Today's course appears above the guide's head as "Today: ...". Personal bests and record times are kept per course. Chimes ring at the checkpoint Braziers and the summit. What lies beyond the summit (the Sky Capital Arc) opens in the full release.

  • Spell Effects: "More" adds richer lights and particles to spells, Weapon Techniques, Chains, hits, and other effects. "Fewer" reduces the effects. Your choice is saved.
  • Hide Headgear (hides hats and helmets): Hides only the appearance of your headgear while keeping its stats.
  • Enable Sound / Master Volume / BGM / Sound Effects / Voice (skill name callouts): Set "Voice" to 0 to turn off the spoken names of techniques and spells.
  • Show Beginner Tips at the bottom of the screen: Control hints (Tips) scroll along the bottom of the screen. This is separate from "Beginner Guidance", which appears at the top for each situation. Once you're used to the controls, clear the checkbox to hide them.
  • Show active quests at bottom: Scrolls your active quests and their progress along the bottom of the screen. It can be turned on and off separately from the Beginner's Guide; with both on, they alternate.
  • Read Beginner's Guide aloud: Reads the hints at the bottom of the screen aloud.
  • Language: Choose between Japanese and English. The display switches as soon as you choose.

Troubleshooting

  • Change password: In the game, go to Main Menu → Settings. On the Web Portal, select "Change password" on the character selection screen or in the top bar.
  • Can't connect: Check that you're connected to the internet, wait a little, and restart the game. During maintenance, a notice appears on the title screen. If it still doesn't work, restart your PC.
  • I installed Cloudflare WARP or ZeroTier before: They're no longer needed. Your account and characters still work as before. Feel free to uninstall them from "Settings → Apps" in Windows.
  • The game closes right away: Update your graphics driver to the latest version.
  • The game is slow or choppy: Go to Main Menu → Settings and lower "Graphics Quality" one step at a time, or use "Auto-Adjust" to match it to your PC again.
  • The text is in Japanese: Go to Main Menu → Settings and set "Language" to "English" to switch back.
  • The Main Menu or Controls List won't open: For the Main Menu, close all windows first and then press Esc. The Controls List opens with , (comma).
  • Neither keys nor mouse work: Press Esc a few times to close all windows. While typing in chat (after /), keys type letters.
  • The attack button doesn't work: You can't act while affected by sleep, stun, or fear. The reason appears on screen.
  • "Another adventurer is fighting it": That monster is someone else's prey. Pick another monster or form a party.
  • Can't cast magic: You're holding an instrument (Bard), you're silenced, you're out of MP, or your Sub Job level is too low.
  • Can't call Bonds: You can't call them during combat, while riding, in the Arena, in the Sky Corridor, or inside Shadow Rockhill. You'll also be refused if the same Bond is already nearby.
  • The update failed: Your previous version is still intact. Launch the game again to retry the download.
  • I can't level past Lv60: During Early Access, Lv60 is the cap and you gain no EXP beyond it. You'll be able to reach Lv61–70 in the full release. If you logged out in the Sky Capital, you'll wake up in town.
