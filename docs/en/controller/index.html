Choosing Party Members (D-pad)

When no window is open, use the D-pad to choose who receives your healing and buff spells (your support target). Your aim at the enemy stays locked, so you can heal and get straight back to attacking.

  • ↑ ↓ Step through your party members one at a time (in the same order as F1–F6 on the keyboard)
  • ← Choose yourself (F1)
  • → Clear the party member selection
In the party list on the left of the screen, the frame of the selected member lights up.

Actions Without a Button

Resting, fishing, the quest list, status, skills, friends, Area Scan, the Card Album, and more can all be opened from the Y Main Menu.

Please use the keyboard to type chat messages.

Supported Controllers

  • Xbox-style (XInput) gamepads that work on Windows. This includes Xbox controllers and many PC gamepads.
  • PlayStation controllers may not work with Windows' default settings. Please use a method that makes them act as an Xbox-style controller, such as launching through Steam.
