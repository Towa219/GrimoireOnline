Grion Fish Guide

A guide to the fish of Grion. Where each one bites, which rod and bait to use, the time and weather it likes, its price, and what it can be crafted into.

For version 1.5.766 · Updated November 18, 2026📗 Back to the Guide日本語

How to Read This Guide

  • Buy a fishing rod and bait at the item shop, then press N (or /fish) at the water's edge to start fishing.
  • Rod: if your Rod Power is too low for the fish, the line snaps. Use the rod shown or a stronger one.
  • Bait, time, weather, weekday: these match the hints in your Angler's Notebook. "Only" means the fish bites only then; "Favored" means it bites more often then.
  • Fish Wholesaler: the Fish Wholesaler in town buys your catch. Today's featured fish changes daily and fetches a higher price. Purchase limits reset after about a week.
  • Legendary Fish: bait and times are not listed. Follow the rumors in your Angler's Notebook to find them.

The numbers are generated automatically from the game's data tables. Contents may change with game updates.
